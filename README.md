## Description

This repository contains the material for the SAP TechEd 2026 session called XP163 - SAP Business AI Platform: Explore, estimate, execute.

## Overview

This session introduces attendees to ways to explore SAP Business AI Platform and to estimate and execute your solutions.


## Requirements

Access to SAP Disovery Center

## Exercises

1. Open https://sap.com/disco
2. Log-on
3. Search for "teched"
4. Start Mission "Explore SAP Business AI Solutions with Discovery Center at TechEd 2026"
5. In the Mission overview, go to tab "Project Board" and follow the instructions, beginning with the upper left card.
